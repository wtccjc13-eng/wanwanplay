Orders ship from France to France and the rest of our supported European delivery area: the European Union, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and Norway. Estimated delivery time is 2–4 days.

Delays

If your parcel has not arrived within 15 business days of dispatch, email play@wanwanplay.com with your order reference. We will check with the carrier.

Lost or damaged parcels

If the carrier loses your parcel or it arrives damaged, we will resend it or refund you in full, at your choice. Please report a damaged parcel within 14 days of delivery and include a photo.

Withdrawal & returns

Your right to withdraw

If you are a consumer in the European Union, you may withdraw from your order within 14 days, without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day you, or a person you name, receive the goods. If you order several games delivered separately, the period starts when you receive the last one.

How to withdraw

Tell us clearly by email at play@wanwanplay.com, or use the notice below. You may also send your notice by post to 大安區光復南路102號4樓, 臺北市, 台灣, 106. Send the game back within 14 days of telling us.

Return costs & condition

You pay the cost of returning the goods. The game must be returned unused and complete, in its fully packaged box.

Refunds

We refund the full price, including standard delivery costs, to the card or wallet you paid with, within 14 days of receiving the goods back or proof that you sent them, whichever is earlier. We never charge a fee for the refund.

Damaged or wrong items

Contact play@wanwanplay.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will replace the item or refund you, including shipping, at no cost to you.

Withdrawal notice — information to include

Send your notice to play@wanwanplay.com or the registered postal address above. Include a clear statement that you wish to withdraw, your order reference, the goods concerned, the order or delivery date, your name and address, and the date of your notice. A paper notice may be signed.
